# Changelog

## 1.3.0

- Companions from the pet collection can have a nickname too: summon one and type `/petname <name>`. With a combat pet out as well, target the companion first.
- Only your own companion is renamed, other players' companions of the same kind keep their names.
- `/petname list` shows companions as well
- `/petname check` shows what PetNickname sees on your target, for bug reports
- Fixed: a nickname that is part of the real name, like Panda for Panda Cub, did not show

## 1.2.0

- Works with Blizzard's default unit frames and other unit frame addons, not just EllesmereUI. Unit frames are found by the unit they show, so no frame names are needed.
- Author is now Hydrax

## 1.1.0

- English texts and description
- Voidwalker icon in the addon list

## 1.0.0

- First version: nicknames on EllesmereUI unit frames, nameplate and tooltip
- `/petname`, `/petname reset`, `/petname list`
